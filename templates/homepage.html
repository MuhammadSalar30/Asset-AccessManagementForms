How it works

From request to approval in four steps

1

Submit your request

Fill in the Assets & Access form online, or ask for a folder or file in a separate request.

2

HOD reviews

Your department HOD approves or rejects, and decides Full Access or Read Only for each folder.

3

Administrator approves

After the HOD's approval, an administrator gives the final OK and the request is completed.

4

You are notified

You receive an email after each decision, with any comments and the folder access granted.

Features

Everything in one system

Digital request form

The paper Assets & Access form, online and always up to date.

Two-level approval

Supervisor / HOD first, then the administrator, with a full approval history.

Email notifications

HODs and administrators are alerted when a request needs them, and employees are told every decision.

Asset records

Every employee has a printable profile of the laptop and access recorded for them.

Folder & file requests

Choose from the shared folders approved by IT, and let your HOD set Full Access or Read Only.

Role-based access

Employees, HODs and administrators each see only what they need.

Who uses it

Built for three roles

Employees

  • Submit asset and access requests
  • Request folders and files
  • View their own asset profile

HODs / Supervisors

  • Approve or reject department requests
  • Set Full Access or Read Only per folder
  • See their department's team and responses

Administrators

  • Give the final approval
  • Manage users, departments and forms
  • Track laptops by brand and department
